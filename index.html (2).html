Questions

The things owners ask on the call.

Do I need to already have a list?

If you take card payments, bookings or online orders, you almost certainly have one. Most owners are surprised how many addresses are already sitting in their POS. If you truly have nothing, collecting them is step one and it's part of setup.

Won't this annoy my customers?

It would if I sent a discount every three days, which is exactly why most lists go dead. Two to four emails a month that are actually worth opening is the whole approach. Unsubscribes stay low because the emails aren't junk.

Who owns the account and the list?

You do, from day one. The account is in your name with your card on it. If you stop working with me you keep the list, the flows and every email I wrote.

How long until it does anything?

The welcome and win-back flows start working the day they go live, because they trigger on their own rather than waiting for a send. Campaign revenue shows up in the first full month.

I already pay for Mailchimp. Do I have to switch?

No. I'll work inside whatever you already pay for. If the tool is genuinely holding you back I'll tell you why and what it would cost to move, then leave the decision to you.

Am I locked into anything?

No contract and no minimum term. Thirty days' notice ends it. Setup-only clients pay once and walk away with the whole thing built.
